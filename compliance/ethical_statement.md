# Ethical Statement

---

## 1. Purpose of the Project

This project is developed for:

- Academic research
- Blockchain experimentation
- AI transparency study
- Financial systems innovation

It is not intended for unauthorized market manipulation.

---

## 2. Responsible AI Use

The AI system:

- Does not exploit insider data
- Does not engage in market spoofing
- Does not generate manipulative signals
- Operates only on public market data

---

## 3. Transparency Principles

The protocol promotes:

- Verifiable execution
- Immutable audit logs
- Open smart contract logic
- Risk-enforced governance

---

## 4. Fair Market Conduct

The system explicitly avoids:

- Latency arbitrage
- Order flooding
- Wash trading simulation
- Artificial liquidity creation

---

## 5. Compliance Awareness

This system acknowledges:

- The authority of SEBI
- Exchange-level trading rules
- Broker-mediated execution requirements

It does not attempt to bypass regulatory oversight.

---

## 6. Academic Integrity

All research findings, metrics, and backtesting results
are documented transparently.

No performance manipulation is performed.

---

## 7. Long-Term Vision

The ethical goal is to explore:

- Transparent trading infrastructure
- On-chain auditability
- Responsible AI deployment in finance
- Decentralized risk governance

The system is designed to advance research, not evade regulation.
